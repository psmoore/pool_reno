Northwest Pool

Main pool · training pool · water fitness from September

An 8-lane, 25-yard main pool and a 3-foot training pool with zero-depth entry. 2925 Apollo Way · 775-334-2203.

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →  —  Use the Pools menu to switch between the main and training pools.

Closures on the day they happen

Northwest has three notices this fall: closed September 23–25 for a maintenance repair, a swim clinic on Sunday, September 27, and closed October 6 for a planned NV Energy power outage. They live in a table on the aquatics page, apart from the schedules they change.

Page to any of those days and the calendar shows it: the closure in place of the sessions it cancels, and every other day exactly as the schedule reads.

Questions for the aquatics office

Everything here was transcribed from what Reno publishes. These are the places where the sources disagree, go quiet, or where we had to make a call — each one is also written on the calendar entry itself.

  • ConflictMoana Springs weekend lap swim: which times are right?
    The Lap Swim Schedule PDF and the Moana Springs page disagree three times. Saturday's 5–10-lane block ends at 12:30 in the PDF and 12:00 on the page; Saturday's 6–8-lane block starts at 1:00 in the PDF and 12:30 on the page; Sunday's first block ends at 2:30 in the PDF and 3:00 on the page. The calendar follows the PDF.
  • GapThe September 27 swim clinic at Northwest
    The closure notice lists a special event, a swim clinic, with no hours. Which Sunday sessions does it replace — lap swim, open swim, Deep Water Fitness — and for how long?
  • GapNorthwest water fitness: which pool, and from when?
    The Northwest page says water fitness “starts September 2026” but gives no date and names no pool. We put Water Fitness and Deep Water Fitness in the main pool and Shallow Water Fitness in the 3-foot training pool, from the first week of September.
  • ConflictNorthwest's midday class: 11:30 or 11:45?
    The Water Fitness page starts the Wednesday and Friday midday class at 11:30; the Northwest facility page says 11:45 (both agree on 11:45 for Monday). The calendar uses 11:45.
  • OursLane ranges shown at their minimum
    The lap schedule gives most blocks as a range, like “(8–10)” or “(2–10)”. The calendar shows the lanes that are always open. When do the others open to lap swim? Say, and the calendar shows each extra lane at the hour it opens.
  • OursWhich Moana lanes do the competition-pool classes use?
    Water Walking and Water Fit name the competition pool but not the lanes. We put them on lanes 16–19, away from lap swim.
  • ConflictEvelyn Mount Northeast: 130 or 1301 Valley Road?
    The Lap Swim Schedule PDF gives “130 Valley Rd.”; the Open Swim PDF and the facility page give 1301. Probably a typo in the lap PDF.
  • GapWhat day in June does the fall–spring schedule end?
    Each facility page gives “August 10, 2026 – June 2027”. With a date, the calendar ends the season on it and the summer schedule can go in beside it now.
