Water Fitness

35 classes a week · three pools

Water Fitness, Deep and Shallow Water Fitness, Water Walking, Water Fit and Aqua Joints at Moana Springs, Northwest Pool and Evelyn Mount Northeast. No swimming skills needed; adults 18+. $6.50 drop-in; $5.00 for seniors.

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →  —  Use the Facilities menu to show one pool.

One schedule, kept in two places

The classes are listed twice: in a table per pool on the Water Fitness page, and again in each facility's “Expand Details” panel. The two agree everywhere but one class — Northwest's midday Wednesday and Friday session starts at 11:30 on one and 11:45 on the other.

On the calendar each class exists once, so the time a swimmer reads is the time that was entered, whichever page they came from.
